yt-arhiva

A private, single-user tool that uploads the owner's own video recordings to the owner's own YouTube channel as private videos, as an off-site backup copy.

It is not offered to anyone else and has no other users.

Privacy policy

Privatni alat za jednog korisnika: vlastite snimke šalje na vlastiti YouTube kanal kao privatne videe, kao drugu kopiju arhive. Pravila privatnosti.
